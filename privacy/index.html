Primissimo

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

Short version

Primissimo does not have accounts, advertising or tracking of any kind. Your setlists, song notes, recordings and photos stay on your device and in the folder you choose. Two things can leave your device: a message you decide to write to us through the feedback form, and, once a day, a short anonymous report about how the app is used. The report carries no identifier and nothing you wrote, and you can switch it off in Settings.

Who is responsible

Primissimo is made by an individual developer. Contact: nestcaesar@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The app stores on your device, or in the folder you choose (including a folder synchronised by a cloud provider you already use):

  • setlists, songs, tempo settings and song notes;
  • images and PDF pages you attach, and handwritten annotations over them;
  • audio recordings you make as song notes;
  • app settings and a few local counters, such as how many days you have played and whether a rating prompt has been shown.

The app asks for microphone access only to record audio notes, and for camera access only when you take a photo for a song note. That content is written to your library and is never sent to us. If your library folder is synchronised by a cloud provider, that provider's own terms apply to it.

When you write to us

The feedback form inside the app sends us only what you put in it:

  • Your message. Required.
  • Your email address. Optional. We ask for it only so that we can reply. Leave it empty and we will still read what you wrote.
  • Technical details. Optional, shown to you in full before you send: app version and build number, platform, operating system version, interface language, and a random identifier generated on your device for this installation. These details contain no song titles, file names or library contents, and the identifier is not linked to you, to your device identifiers, or to any advertising identifier.

We use this only to understand the problem, to fix it, and to reply to you. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we never sell it or pass it to anyone other than the service providers listed below.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom: the legal basis for processing your message and your optional email address is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by sending the form, and you may withdraw it at any time by asking us to delete the message. The legal basis for the abuse protection described below is our legitimate interest in keeping the service usable (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Abuse protection

When your message reaches our server, we compute a one-way hash of your IP address together with a secret value and store only that hash. It lets us limit how many messages can be sent from one connection per hour. Your IP address itself is not stored, and the hash cannot be turned back into it.

If you have no connection

A message you send without a connection is kept on your device, in the app's own folder, and is sent the next time the app starts. It never leaves your device before that.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which devices and features matter, the app sends a short report to our server at most once a day while it is in use. You can turn this off at any time: Settings → About → Anonymous statistics. With the switch off, nothing is sent.

The report contains:

  • app version and build, whether it came from a store, and whether Pro features are unlocked;
  • platform, device model (such as iPad14,1) and operating system version;
  • system language and region, and the language chosen in the app;
  • appearance and accessibility: light or dark theme, the system text size, the screen size class, and whether a screen reader such as VoiceOver was on;
  • the values of app settings, such as the metronome sound and the text sizes you chose;
  • the size of your library as a range (for example “10–49 songs”), never an exact count;
  • how many times features were used since the previous report, how many minutes the app was on screen and how many minutes the metronome played;
  • the month the app was first started, and whether this is the first report of the week and of the month. This is how we count active installations without identifying any of them.

When the report arrives, our server adds the country, region, city and time zone that Cloudflare derives from the network address of the connection. The app never asks for your location and does not use location services.

The report does not contain, and we do not store with it: any installation, device or advertising identifier; your IP address or its hash; the name of your device; song or setlist titles, file names, folder paths, note text, recordings or images. Reports are not linked to each other, to a feedback message, or to you, so we cannot tell which reports came from the same person.

To limit abuse, the server keeps a one-way hash of the sender's IP address in a separate counter that is erased every day and is not connected to the reports.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom: we do not consider these reports personal data, because they cannot be attributed to an identifiable person. To the extent the law treats them as such, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), and you can object at any time by turning the switch off. Because reports carry no identifier, we cannot find, export or delete the reports of a particular person.

Rating prompts

The app may ask you to rate it using the rating window provided by Apple or Google. That window belongs to the store, not to us: we do not learn whether you rated the app, what rating you gave, or whether the window was shown at all. What you write in a public store review is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. The decision about when to show the prompt is made on your device, from local counters that are never sent anywhere.

This website

The website has no cookies, no accounts and no advertising. To see how many people visit it, each page you open sends one short anonymous record to our server:

  • which of our site addresses and which page you opened, and its language;
  • whether you saw the light or the dark theme, and whether you chose it with the theme switch;
  • the screen size class and the operating system family (such as iOS or Windows), taken from your browser's User-Agent, which itself is not stored;
  • the domain of the site that linked to ours, if any (never the full address);
  • whether this is the first page you opened in this browser tab, which is how we count visitors without identifying them. The browser remembers this only until the tab is closed.

Our server adds the country, region and city that Cloudflare derives from the network address of the connection. As with app reports, we do not store your IP address or any identifier, the records are not linked to each other or to you, and a one-way hash of the address is kept in a separate counter for one day to limit abuse.

The website also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused service that counts page views without cookies and without fingerprinting.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom: the legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who else processes your data

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the service that receives messages, usage reports and website visit records, and the database that stores them, and provides Cloudflare Web Analytics for the website. Processing happens in Cloudflare's global network.
  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the website, and Cloudflare may also serve it. Like any web server, they receive your IP address when your browser loads a page; we do not receive their logs.
  • Telegram Messenger Inc. delivers a notification to the developer when a message arrives. That notification contains your message, and your email address if you provided one.

All of them act as our service providers under their own privacy terms. There are no other recipients, and no data is sold or shared for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Messages are deleted 12 months after they arrive.
  • Your email address is removed 90 days after we answer you.
  • You can ask us to delete your message earlier — see below.
  • Anonymous usage reports and website visit records are deleted 25 months after they arrive; the abuse-protection counter for them is erased daily.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or restrict how we use it. Write to nestcaesar@gmail.com. If you quote the eight-character reference number shown after you sent a message, we can find it immediately; without it, we may not be able to connect a message to you at all, because a message sent without an email address is not linked to any identity.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Primissimo is a tool for musicians and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us a message, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Messages travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and are stored in a database that only the developer can reach, behind an authenticated administration page. No system is perfect, but we keep what we store to the minimum described above precisely so that there is little to lose.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
